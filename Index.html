our story

Grown slowly, whisked gently

Moss & Matcha began in a tiny kitchen with a single bamboo whisk and a big love for slow mornings. Our leaves are hand-picked from a small family farm in Uji, Japan, then stone-ground in small batches so every tin arrives fresh and vivid green.

We believe matcha isn't a rush — it's a ritual. A warm bowl, a quiet moment, and the softest start to your day.
